# Social Analyst Refrac

Social Analyst Refrac turns the social lane into a bounded market-mood and
asset-attention pipeline. The goal is to keep social evidence separate from
news catalysts and avoid treating noisy social inputs as truth or trade
instructions.

## Documents

- [V1 implementation plan](v1/implementation-plan.md)

## Version Scope

- V1: macro Fear & Greed context, asset-specific CoinGecko trending/social
  attention, structured social context, deterministic quality labels, and
  prompt/report guardrails.
- V1.2: influencer/official posts as optional narrative evidence only. This is
  intentionally deferred because it needs provenance, author allowlists,
  credibility scoring, manipulation-risk handling, and prompt-injection guards.
